# Pulse Chat

A mobile-first messaging and social app for people outside the organization. Anyone can sign in with an email link, set up a profile, and then:

- Chat one-to-one or in groups, sharing text, photos, videos and view-once media
- Make voice and video calls from any chat
- Post updates with photos or videos to a shared feed, with likes and comments

It can be added to an Android or iPhone home screen from the browser.
